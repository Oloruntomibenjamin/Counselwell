Our services

Support for different seasons of life

Sessions are collaborative and shaped around what matters to you. We’ll discuss your preferences and help you decide on a next step.

Individual counselling

A confidential space to explore stress, life changes, relationships, or personal goals with a therapist.

Good to know: You can begin with a conversation about what brings you in and what you hope will change.

Couples counselling

Guided conversations for partners who want to understand each other better and work through recurring challenges.

Focus areas: Communication, connection, boundaries, and shared decisions.

Groups and workshops

Small-group learning on practical wellbeing skills, coping strategies, and building supportive connections.

Format: Topics and schedules can vary; ask us about current and upcoming groups.

Online sessions

Where available, meet remotely from a private space that works for you, without travelling to an office.

Before you book: Ask about availability, privacy, and whether remote sessions are appropriate for your situation.

Support for young people

Age-appropriate support that takes a young person’s comfort, needs, and family context into account.

Next step: Contact us to discuss age ranges, consent, and the services currently offered.

Relationship support

Space to reflect on patterns, strengthen communication, and consider practical ways forward—together or individually.

Your pace: We’ll agree on a focus and review what feels useful as you go.

What to expect from a first conversation

Start with your priorities

Tell us what you’re looking for, what feels important, and any preferences you have about support.

Explore possible options

We can explain the formats currently available and answer practical questions before you decide.

Choose your next step

You can take time to think. If you decide to proceed, we’ll discuss a suitable appointment and what happens next.
