For couples preparing for marriage

Premarital and wedding counselling

Getting ready for marriage can bring excitement as well as important conversations. Counselling gives intending and engaged couples a supportive space to talk about the life they want to build together.

Premarital counselling

Explore expectations and strengthen the skills that can support a healthy partnership before marriage.

  • Communication and listening
  • Values, goals, and expectations
  • Handling disagreements constructively
  • Family, finances, and shared responsibilities

Wedding-season support

Make space for your relationship while navigating the pressure and decisions that can come with planning a wedding.

  • Managing stress and competing priorities
  • Talking through family expectations and boundaries
  • Making decisions as a team
  • Keeping connection and shared values in focus

Sessions are for relationship support, not wedding planning. Contact us to ask about availability and whether this service is right for you.
