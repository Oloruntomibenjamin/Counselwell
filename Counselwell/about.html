About CounselWell

A thoughtful place to begin.

Reaching out can take courage. Our aim is to make the first steps clearer, answer your questions, and help you explore support at a pace that feels manageable.

Start a conversation →
What guides us

Care that respects your story

Our approach is grounded in listening, collaboration, and respect for each person’s circumstances. We’ll work with you to understand what matters and discuss suitable options.

Listen first

Your perspective and priorities guide the conversation. You can share only what feels comfortable, at your own pace.

Work together

We’ll discuss what you hope to get from support and check in about whether the approach feels useful.

Respect your choices

You can ask questions, consider different options, and decide what you want to do next.

How getting started works

1. Explore

Read about services and consider what kind of support you may be looking for.

2. Reach out

Send a message with your questions or request information about available options.

3. Talk it through

Discuss practical details and possible next steps before deciding whether to book.

A welcoming conversation

We’ll explain what to expect and leave space for the questions that matter to you.

A comfortable setting

Ask us about where sessions take place and what remote options may be available.

Your pace matters

There’s no need to have everything figured out before you make contact.

Want to know more?

We’re happy to answer questions about the practice and the support options available.
